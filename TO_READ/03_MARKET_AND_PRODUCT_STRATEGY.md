# Market & Product Strategy — LensCraft AI

## Target Audience

### Primary Ideal Customer Profile (ICP)
**Independent Music Artists & Producers** aged 18-35 in Francophone markets (Burkina Faso, Côte d'Ivoire, Senegal, France) who:
- Produce their own music or collaborate with local producers
- Need professional video treatment documentation for pitch meetings with labels, sponsors, or production teams
- Lack access to expensive pre-production consultants
- Are active on social media (TikTok, Instagram Reels) and understand short-form video
- Budget: $0-$200/month for creative tools

### Secondary Audiences
| Segment | Size | Willingness to Pay |
|---------|------|-------------------|
| Music video directors / DOPs | Medium | High ($50-200/project for client-facing docs) |
| Content creators / influencers | Large | Low-Medium ($5-10/mo for promo concepts) |
| Film students | Small | Very Low (willing to use free tier only) |
| Record labels (small/indie) | Small | Medium (team plans for multiple projects) |

## The Problem They Face

Creating a professional music video treatment dossier traditionally requires:
1. A creative director to conceptualize visuals
2. A screenwriter to draft the scenario
3. A DP to plan shot lists and camera directions
4. A production manager to schedule shooting days
5. A marketing strategist to plan promotion

This costs **$2,000-$15,000+** and takes **weeks**. Independent artists cannot afford this.

LensCraft AI automates this entire process into a single input → comprehensive output workflow at a fraction of the cost.

## Existing Alternatives

| Alternative | What It Does | Gap vs LensCraft |
|------------|--------------|------------------|
| Manual consultation services | Custom treatments from freelancers on Malt/Fiverr | Expensive, slow, non-standardized |
| Canva/CapCut templates | Visual templates for video editing | No script/shot list/schedule generation |
| ChatGPT/Claude for prompts | General-purpose text generation | No structured schema enforcement, no audio analysis, no multi-section pipeline |
| Adobe Premiere/Final Cut | Video editing software | Does not produce pre-production documents |
| ShotDesigner / Showbiz APP | Shot list management | Post-hoc planning tool, no AI content generation |

**Differentiation:** LensCraft is the only product that combines **audio understanding** + **structured multi-section dossier generation** + **social media promo strategy** in one workflow specifically designed for **music video production**.

## Positioning Statement

> "For independent music artists who need professional video treatment documentation, LensCraft AI is an AI-powered platform that generates complete production dossiers — from song analysis to shot lists, shooting schedules, and viral promo strategies — in minutes, not weeks."

## Value Proposition (One Sentence)

Generate a complete, exportable music video production dossier and social media promo strategy in under 5 minutes from just your song's audio, lyrics, or concept.

## Core Benefits

1. **Time Savings:** Go from raw song to full production dossier in ~5 minutes instead of weeks of consulting
2. **Professional Quality:** Structured output matching what production teams expect (script, shot list, schedule, promo)
3. **Cost Efficiency:** $9.99/month vs $2,000+ traditional consultant fees
4. **Viral-Ready Promo:** Built-in TikTok/Reels/Shorts strategy generator with virality scoring
5. **Multi-Language:** Supports Francophone African markets plus global English/Spanish
